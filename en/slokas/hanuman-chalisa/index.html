🐒 Hanuman Chalisa

The Hanuman Chalisa was composed by Goswami Tulsidas in the 16th century, in the Awadhi language. Chalisa means "forty" — forty verses, framed by three dohas (couplets).

It is perhaps the most recited devotional hymn in India. People chant it for courage, for protection, and in difficulty.

Here every verse is given in Roman script so you can chant it, with its meaning in English.

📖 Verses

Opening Dohas Verses 1–10 Verses 11–20 Verses 21–30 Verses 31–40 Closing Doha

Opening Dohas

1
Shri Guru Charan Saroj Raj, Nija manu Mukuru Sudhaari |
Baranau Raghubar Bimal Jasu, Jo Daayaku Phal Chaari ||
Meaning: Cleansing the mirror of my mind with the dust from my Guru's lotus feet, I sing the pure glory of Sri Rama — the giver of the four fruits of life (dharma, artha, kama, moksha).
Note: Tulsidas begins not with Hanuman, but with his guru — and with humility. The mind is a mirror; devotion is what wipes it clean.
2
Buddhi Heen Tanu Jaanike, Sumirau Pavan Kumaar |
Bal Buddhi Vidya Dehu Mohi, Harahu Kalesa Bikaar ||
Meaning: Knowing myself to be without wisdom, I remember you, O son of the Wind. Grant me strength, intelligence and knowledge — and remove my sorrows and flaws.
Note: The honest prayer. He doesn't claim to be worthy. He says plainly: I have no wisdom. That admission is where devotion begins.

Verses 1 – 10

3
Jai Hanuman gyaan gun saagar |
Jai Kapees tihun lok ujaagar ||
Meaning: Victory to Hanuman, ocean of wisdom and virtue! Victory to the Lord of monkeys, who illuminates all three worlds.
4
Raam doot atulit bal dhaamaa |
Anjani-putra Pavanasut naamaa ||
Meaning: You are Rama's messenger, the abode of immeasurable strength. You are known as Anjani's son and the Son of the Wind.
5
Mahaabeer Bikram Bajrangee |
Kumati nivaar sumati ke sangee ||
Meaning: Great hero, mighty as a thunderbolt, with limbs like a diamond. You dispel evil thoughts and are the companion of good sense.
Note: Bajrangi — 'vajra-limbed'. His body is as hard as a thunderbolt, yet his role here is gentle: to remove kumati (bad thinking).
6
Kanchan baran biraaj subesaa |
Kaanan kundal kunchit kesaa ||
Meaning: Golden-hued and splendidly adorned, with earrings and beautiful curly hair.
7
Haath bajra au dhvajaa biraajai |
Kaandhe moonj janeu saajai ||
Meaning: In your hands shine the mace and the flag; the sacred thread of munja grass adorns your shoulder.
8
Shankar suvan Kesari Nandan |
Tej prataap mahaa jag vandan ||
Meaning: You are an incarnation of Shiva and the son of Kesari. Your radiance and glory are worshipped by the whole world.
9
Vidyaavaan gunee ati chaatur |
Raam kaaj karibe ko aatur ||
Meaning: You are learned, virtuous and exceedingly clever — always eager to do Rama's work.
Note: Notice the order. Learning and wisdom come before eagerness. Hanuman is not merely strong; he is intelligent, and Rama praised him for his speech.
10
Prabhu charitra sunibe ko rasiyaa |
Raam Lakhan Seeta man basiyaa ||
Meaning: You delight in listening to the Lord's story. Rama, Lakshmana and Sita dwell in your heart.
11
Sookshma roop dhari Siyahi dikhaawaa |
Bikat roop dhari Lank jaraawaa ||
Meaning: Taking a tiny form you appeared before Sita; taking a fearsome form you burned Lanka.
Note: The same being becomes small enough to sit unseen in a tree, and vast enough to set a city alight. Power is knowing which form the moment requires.
12
Bheem roop dhari asur sanhaare |
Raamachandra ke kaaj sanvaare ||
Meaning: Assuming a mighty form you destroyed the demons and accomplished Rama's tasks.

Verses 11 – 20

13
Laaye Sajeevan Lakhan jiyaaye |
Shri Raghubeer harashi ur laaye ||
Meaning: You brought the Sanjeevani herb and revived Lakshmana; Sri Rama embraced you with joy.
Note: He didn't know which herb it was — so he carried back the entire mountain. When in doubt, Hanuman does not do half a job.
14
Raghupati keenhee bahut badaayee |
Tum mam priya Bharat-hi sam bhaayee ||
Meaning: Rama praised you greatly, saying: 'You are as dear to me as my brother Bharata.'
15
Sahas badan tumharo jas gaavai |
As kahi Shripati kanth lagaavai ||
Meaning: 'The thousand-headed Shesha sings your glory' — saying this, the Lord of Lakshmi embraced you.
16
Sanakaadik Brahmaadi Muneesaa |
Naarad Saarad sahit Aheesaa ||
Meaning: Sanaka and the sages, Brahma and the great rishis, Narada, Saraswati and the King of Serpents —
17
Jam Kuber Digpaal jahaan te |
Kavi kovid kahi sake kahaan te ||
Meaning: Yama, Kubera and the guardians of the directions — even poets and scholars cannot fully describe your glory.
18
Tum upkaar Sugreevahin keenhaa |
Raam milaay raaj pad deenhaa ||
Meaning: You did a great service to Sugriva — you united him with Rama, and he regained his kingdom.
19
Tumharo mantra Vibheeshan maanaa |
Lankeshwar bhaye sab jag jaanaa ||
Meaning: Vibhishana heeded your counsel and became the Lord of Lanka — as all the world knows.
20
Jug sahasra jojan par Bhaanu |
Leelyo taahi madhur phal jaanu ||
Meaning: The sun lies thousands of yojanas away — yet you swallowed it, thinking it a sweet fruit.
Note: The famous verse about baby Hanuman leaping for the sun. A striking image of a child's fearlessness.
21
Prabhu mudrikaa meli mukh maaheen |
Jaladhi laanghi gaye achraj naaheen ||
Meaning: Carrying the Lord's ring in your mouth, you leapt across the ocean — no wonder in that.
Note: 'No wonder in that' — Tulsidas's quiet humour. For Hanuman, crossing an ocean is unremarkable.
22
Durgam kaaj jagat ke jete |
Sugam anugrah tumhre tete ||
Meaning: Whatever difficult tasks exist in this world — they become easy by your grace.

Verses 21 – 30

23
Raam duaare tum rakhvaare |
Hot na aagyaa binu paisaare ||
Meaning: You are the guardian at Rama's door; no one enters without your permission.
24
Sab sukh lahai tumhaaree sharnaa |
Tum rakshak kaahu ko dar naa ||
Meaning: All happiness is found in your refuge. When you protect, there is nothing to fear.
25
Aapan tej samhaaro aapai |
Teenon lok haank te kaanpai ||
Meaning: You alone can contain your own power; the three worlds tremble at your roar.
26
Bhoot pisaach nikat nahin aavai |
Mahaabeer jab naam sunaavai ||
Meaning: Evil spirits and demons dare not come near, when the name of the Great Hero is uttered.
27
Naasai rog harai sab peeraa |
Japat nirantar Hanumat Beeraa ||
Meaning: Disease is destroyed and all pain removed, by constantly chanting the name of the brave Hanuman.
Note: An important note: This expresses devotional faith. It is not a substitute for medical care — please see a doctor when you are ill.
28
Sankat te Hanumaan chhudaavai |
Man kram bachan dhyaan jo laavai ||
Meaning: Hanuman frees from every trouble the one who remembers him in thought, word and deed.
29
Sab par Raam tapasvee raajaa |
Tin ke kaaj sakal tum saajaa ||
Meaning: Rama is the supreme ascetic king — and you accomplished all his work.
30
Aur manorath jo koi laavai |
Soi amit jeevan phal paavai ||
Meaning: Whoever brings any desire to you obtains its abundant fruit in this life.
31
Chaaron jug prataap tumhaaraa |
Hai prasiddh jagat ujiyaaraa ||
Meaning: Your glory shines through all four ages; your fame illuminates the world.
32
Saadhu sant ke tum rakhvaare |
Asur nikandan Raam dulaare ||
Meaning: You are the protector of saints and sages, the destroyer of demons, and the beloved of Rama.

Verses 31 – 40

33
Ashta siddhi nav nidhi ke daataa |
As bar deen Jaanakee Maataa ||
Meaning: You can grant the eight powers and the nine treasures — such a boon was given to you by Mother Janaki (Sita).
34
Raam rasaayan tumhare paasaa |
Sadaa raho Raghupati ke daasaa ||
Meaning: You hold the elixir of Rama's devotion; you remain forever the servant of Raghupati.
Note: He was offered every power — and chose to remain a servant. This is the heart of Hanuman: infinite strength, infinite humility.
35
Tumhare bhajan Raam ko paavai |
Janam janam ke dukh bisraavai ||
Meaning: By singing your praise one attains Rama, and the sorrows of many births are forgotten.
36
Ant kaal Raghubar pur jaayee |
Jahaan janma Hari-bhakt kahaayee ||
Meaning: At the end, one goes to Rama's abode — or is born again as a devotee of Hari.
37
Aur devtaa chitt na dharayee |
Hanumat sei sarv sukh karayee ||
Meaning: One need not hold any other deity in mind; serving Hanuman brings all happiness.
38
Sankat katai mitai sab peeraa |
Jo sumirai Hanumat Balbeeraa ||
Meaning: All troubles are cut away and all pain removed, for whoever remembers the mighty Hanuman.
39
Jai Jai Jai Hanumaan Gosaaeen |
Kripaa karahu Gurudev kee naaeen ||
Meaning: Victory, victory, victory to Lord Hanuman! Bestow your grace upon me as my Guru would.
40
Jo shat baar paath kar koee |
Chhootahi bandi mahaa sukh hoee ||
Meaning: Whoever recites this a hundred times is freed from bondage and attains great happiness.
41
Jo yah padhai Hanumaan Chaaleesaa |
Hoy siddhi saakhee Gaureesaa ||
Meaning: Whoever reads this Hanuman Chalisa attains success — Lord Shiva himself is witness to this.
42
Tulasidaas sadaa Hari cheraa |
Keejai Naath hridaya mah deraa ||
Meaning: Tulsidas is forever the servant of Hari. O Lord, please make your dwelling in my heart.
Note: The poet signs his name — and asks for nothing but presence. Not wealth, not power. Only: live in my heart.

Closing Doha

43
Pavan Tanay Sankat Harana, Mangal Moorati Roop |
Raam Lakhan Seetaa Sahit, Hriday Basahu Sur Bhoop ||
Meaning: O Son of the Wind, remover of sorrows, embodiment of auspiciousness — together with Rama, Lakshmana and Sita, please dwell in my heart, O King of Gods.
Note: How to recite: Traditionally on Tuesday or Saturday. Bathe, light a lamp before Hanuman, and recite with a calm mind.

But the Chalisa itself says the only requirement is man kram bachan — thought, word and deed. Sincerity matters more than ritual.
📚 Source: Hanuman Chalisa by Goswami Tulsidas (Awadhi original). Transliteration and translation in our own words.
